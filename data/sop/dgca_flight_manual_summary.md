## A. General Operating Limits

A.1 Maximum height: 120 m (400 ft) above ground level in the green zone unless specifically permitted.

A.2 Operations must stay within the permitted area for the flight. Do not breach geofences.

A.3 Yellow-zone and red-zone operations need prior permission. Critical infrastructure such as power substations may need permission from the facility owner and local authority.

A.4 The Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC) is responsible for the safe conduct of every flight, including autonomous segments.

## B. Remote Pilot Responsibilities

B.1 The RPIC must be able to take manual control or command a Return-to-Launch at any time.

B.2 Autonomous systems that change the flight path must have a clear human override.

B.3 The RPIC must keep a flight log with take-off/landing times, location, incidents and any deviation from the plan.

## C. Safety and Failsafes

C.1 Return-to-Launch (RTL) failsafe must be configured and tested before flight.

C.2 Low-battery failsafe: the aircraft must return or land with enough reserve to reach the launch point safely. Operators commonly set the RTL threshold at 20–25% remaining.

C.3 Lost-link failsafe: on loss of command link for more than 5 seconds, the aircraft must RTL or land.

C.4 Do not operate in winds above the manufacturer's limit. For the SkyGuard reference airframe the limit is 25 knots sustained.

## D. Operations Near Hazards

D.1 Keep a safe lateral and vertical separation from high-voltage power lines. Electromagnetic interference near energised lines can corrupt compass readings. Keep at least 15 m from conductors, and more in gusty conditions.

D.2 Do not fly directly over people who are not taking part in the operation, or over moving vehicles, below 30 m.

D.3 Respect privacy. Capture imagery only of the permitted area and use it only for the stated purpose.

## E. Incident Reporting

E.1 Any accident or serious incident, including a fly-away, a geofence breach or an emergency landing, must be reported to the authority and to the operator's accountable manager.

E.2 Security incidents seen during a flight should be reported to the facility security team and, where appropriate, to local police.
